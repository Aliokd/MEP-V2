# Veinote — Launch Commercial (0:50)

**Working title:** *Every civilization built a tool for this*
**Format:** 50s. Master in 16:9, cut down to 9:16 (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) and 1:1 (feed).
**Structure:** Lineage (0:00–0:18) → Break (0:18–0:28) → Veinote (0:28–0:44) → Card (0:44–0:50)
**VO budget:** ~100 words total. That is ~2 words/sec — deliberately slow, leaves air for music and picture.

---

## Voice-over, full read

> Before writing, there was a song.
>
> Every civilization since built a new tool to carry it. Clay. Vellum. Ink. Tape.
> Every one of them made the same promise — help a human say something true.
>
> Then came a tool that skipped the human.
> Now the feed fills with songs nobody wrote. And nobody remembers.
>
> Veinote is the opposite.
> Tools that keep your hands on the song. Lessons from writers with three decades behind them.
> Exercises that build real craft. A community that actually listens.
>
> Your words. Your song. Yours to own.
>
> Veinote. The home of human songwriting.

**Casting:** one voice, close-mic'd, unhurried, no announcer energy. Ideally a songwriter, not a voice actor. A slight accent is an asset — it reads human, which is the whole thesis.

## Sound design is the argument

The film's thesis lives in the audio, not the script. One melody, established as a single unaccompanied human voice at 0:00, gains an instrument per civilization, gets **hard-muted at 0:18**, sits in silence, and returns at 0:28 to finish. If the melody is memorable, the ad works with the picture off.

Licensing: use an original piece written by Peter or a Veinote writer. A stock cue on a film about human authorship is a headline waiting to happen. Get the writer's name in the end credits or the caption.

---

## Claims and legal

- **"92% of musicians never finish their songs"** appears on the site with a source (forskning.no). It is *not* in this script — no room, and a naked stat in an ad invites a fight. If marketing wants it, it goes in the caption with the link, not in the VO.
- **"platinum-selling, top 2% songwriter worldwide"** and **"250+ released songs"** are already public on the site. Keep the on-screen credential card short: name, "platinum-selling songwriter", "250+ released songs".
- **"Yours to own"** is a rights claim. Confirm it matches the ToS before this ships.
- Historical era captions (`c. 1400 BCE` etc.) should be sanity-checked against whatever props/footage you actually source — mismatched artifact and date is the kind of thing a comment section finds in an hour.
- Don't render fake competitor UI that could be mistaken for a real product.
